# ADR-029 — Match modes as ranking strategies

Status: Accepted for Prompt 007.

EXACT, COORDINATED and COMPLEMENTARY share one compatibility retrieval pipeline. Their deterministic neutral ranking strategies use versioned color/pattern rules and explicit weights. COORDINATED is the MVP default. No Complete My Look or other future mode is introduced.

See [matching and ranking foundation](../architecture/matching-ranking-foundation.md) for the exact v1 rules and limitations.
